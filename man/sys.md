# SYS

Systems and airframe at a glance, part of the **AVN** (Avionics) hub: aircraft name, a top-down
silhouette that darkens per-part as it takes damage, with engine-fire and other critical failure
callouts, and a nose-on view of your hardpoints (mirroring the cockpit's own weapon-station panel).

Each hardpoint links to a box naming the weapon loaded on it, in green while it has ammo, red once
it is fired out, and gray when nothing is mounted.

Beside the silhouette, a bank of system toggles — gear, radar, guns, engine, flight assist, night
vision, nav lights, turret — shows live state; tap one to switch that system. GEAR follows the
game's own gear key: it only moves the gear in the air, and not while it is already moving. GUNS
lights whenever the game has guns linked, on any aircraft.

Aircraft whose weapon panel has no stations on the nose-on drawing show a row of pylon squares
under it instead, and those without a nose-on view show just the squares and weapon boxes.

In a narrow pane the toggles move into a band under the aircraft name, and the weapon boxes stack
in columns either side of the nose-on view.
