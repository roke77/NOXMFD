# SYS

Systems at a glance, part of the **AVN** (Avionics) hub: circular gauges for engine RPM, fuel, IR
heat signature, and throttle (with an afterburner range on the dial), plus a bank of system
toggles — gear, radar, guns, engine, flight assist, night vision, nav lights, turret — that show
live state. Tap a toggle on the page to switch that system. GEAR follows the game's own gear key:
it only moves the gear in the air, and not while it is already moving. GUNS lights whenever the
game has guns linked, on any aircraft.
