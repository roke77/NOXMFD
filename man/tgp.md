# TGP

Targeting-pod camera feed zoomed on the locked target, with range and bearing. (Low quality for
now. Follow high quality development [here](https://github.com/roke77/NOXMFD/issues/10))

![TGP page](images/TGP.png)

## Refresh rate

The feed's update rate is one of the two live-adjustable sliders on [RTS](rates.md) — higher
costs more CPU/GPU and network bandwidth, lower saves it at the cost of smoothness and latency.
Defaults to 15 Hz.
