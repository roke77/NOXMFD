# ADI

Attitude at a glance, part of the **AVN** (Avionics) hub, laid out like the game's own flight
instruments panel. Open it from MAIN's **AVN** key; the hub opens on ADI, and **ADI / AFM / HSI /
SYS** switch between its pages.

![ADI page](images/ADI.png)

- **Top**: heading, then true airspeed.
- **Attitude ball** — sky and ground with a pitch ladder (a line every 5°, numbered every 10°), a
  bank scale (10/20/30/45/60°) whose pointer turns with the ball, and the fixed amber aircraft
  symbol. The **slip ball** under it rolls toward the side the aircraft is slipping to.
- **AOA bar** (left) — angle of attack, ±30°.
- **VVI bar** (right) — vertical speed: ±10,000 ft/min (in thousands), or ±50 m/s in metric.
- **Bottom**: climb rate, then altitude above sea level.
- **Corners**: **MACH** and **AOA** on the left, **G** (with the highest G since the mission
  started) and **R-ALT** (radar altitude above the ground) on the right.

Units follow the game's own unit setting: knots, feet and feet per minute, or km/h, metres and
metres per second. AoA shows a dash below taxi speed, as the game's own readout does.

The ball's sky and ground colours are **ADI SKY** and **ADI GROUND** on the [UI](ui.md) page.
