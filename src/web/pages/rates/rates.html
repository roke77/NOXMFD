REFRESH RATES
Higher rates cost more CPU / bandwidth; lower rates save it at the cost of smoothness and latency. Changes apply immediately and persist across restarts.
TLM — TELEMETRY
The main data tick: own-ship state, weapons, MAP/RWR/RDR/MW contacts, TGT filters, BDF/PAL faction stats. Everything on this page's other MFD pages rides this one rate.
10 Hz
TGP — CAMERA FEED
The targeting-pod pane's capture rate. Independent of TLM above.
15 Hz
Above 15 Hz the GPU readback can't keep up on modest hardware — measured up to ~45% of captures dropped at 30 Hz, with frame spikes over 50ms. Raise this only if you have GPU headroom to spend.
TGP — FEED QUALITY
NATIVE (default) reads the game's own targeting-pod camera as-is. HQ renders a separate, higher-resolution camera every frame instead — sharper picture, real tree/grass detail, at the cost of an extra render pass. Still an early-pass feature: no thermal/IR look or on-screen mag/dist readout in HQ yet.
HQ is an early-pass feature — expect rough edges. It renders a second camera every frame and may cost noticeable FPS.
