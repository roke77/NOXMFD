TGP — CONFIG
Higher rates cost more CPU / bandwidth; lower rates save it at the cost of smoothness and latency. Changes apply immediately and persist across restarts.
TGP — REFRESH RATE
The targeting-pod pane's capture rate. Independent of MAP's own telemetry rate (/mapcfg).
15 Hz
Above 15 Hz the GPU readback can't keep up on modest hardware — measured up to ~45% of captures dropped at 30 Hz, with frame spikes over 50ms. Raise this only if you have GPU headroom to spend.
TGP — FEED RESOLUTION
LOW (default) uses the game's native 360×240 camera. MID uses a 720×480 mirror camera. HIGH uses a 1080×720 mirror camera. MID and HIGH render an additional camera and increase readback and encoding work.
MID and HIGH render a second camera and may cost noticeable FPS. HIGH also reads back and encodes 2.25× as many pixels as MID.
TGP — FEED QUALITY
Controls JPEG compression only: LOW uses 30, MID (default) uses 50, and HIGH uses 90. Higher quality retains more fine detail and produces larger MJPEG frames; it does not change the camera resolution.
HIGH retains more detail but increases MJPEG frame size, bandwidth, and encoder pressure.
HIGH resolution with HIGH quality is the maximum GPU, CPU, and bandwidth setting.
TGP — HIDE COCKPIT FEED
When ON, hide the in-cockpit TGP overlay so the cockpit display can show its normal content and the external feed is the only TGP picture.
