TGP — CONFIG
Higher rates cost more CPU / bandwidth; lower rates save it at the cost of smoothness and latency. Changes apply immediately and persist across restarts.
TGP — CAMERA FEED
The targeting-pod pane's capture rate. Independent of MAP's own telemetry rate (/mapcfg).
15 Hz
Above 15 Hz the GPU readback can't keep up on modest hardware — measured up to ~45% of captures dropped at 30 Hz, with frame spikes over 50ms. Raise this only if you have GPU headroom to spend.
TGP — FEED QUALITY
NATIVE (default) reads the game's own targeting-pod camera as-is. HQ renders a separate, higher-resolution camera every frame instead — sharper picture, real tree/grass detail, a mag/range/grid/target-detail overlay with a per-target lock box, and a basic thermal-style IR look, at the cost of an extra render pass. Still an early-pass feature: expect rough edges.
HQ is an early-pass feature — expect rough edges. It renders a second camera every frame and may cost noticeable FPS.
